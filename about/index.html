About Me

Rock mechanics specialist & Python developer focused on engineering automation.

Who I Am

I’m M. Balandari Toroghi, a rock mechanics specialist with hands-on experience in laboratory testing, analysis, and engineering workflows. Over time, I realized how much engineering work can be improved through automation and clean software tools - so I began building my own.

Today, I combine my engineering background with Python development to create practical tools for rock mass classification, UCS/TCS analysis, and reporting. My goal is to make engineering calculations faster, clearer, and more accessible.

What I Do

  • Build engineering tools using Python and PySide6.
  • Automate rock mechanics workflows (RMR, Q-System, GSI).
  • Process and analyze UCS/TCS test data.
  • Create clean, structured reports for engineering documentation.
  • Design GUIs and data visualization tools.

My Approach

I focus on clarity, reliability, and engineering correctness. Every tool I build is designed to be easy to use, transparent in its calculations, and helpful for real-world decision making.
